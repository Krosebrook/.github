# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to keep participation in our open-source repositories harassment-free and grounded in technical evidence for everyone regardless of background, experience level, disability, gender identity, race, religion, or nationality.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code and evidence instead of people.
- Provide reproducible steps, commit SHAs, and clear context when filing issues or reviewing pull requests.
- Accept constructive pushback and correct factual errors promptly when counterexamples surface.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Submitting fabricated test output, plagiarized code, or undisclosed credential payloads.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to `Kyle@abstrekkt.com` with links to the relevant issue, pull request, or comment. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that violate these rules.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
